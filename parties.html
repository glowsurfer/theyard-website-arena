Birthday parties · All four centres · Ages 4 to 14

The party they talk about for weeks.

A Yard birthday party is two hours of the gym to yourselves at any of our four Singapore centres: a 90-minute session run by the coaches who teach our classes all week, then 30 minutes of cake in a party room decorated before you arrive. Gymnastics parties run at every centre, and ninja parties run at Bukit Timah and Dempsey.

Why families book the next one

Real coaches, real apparatus,
and a room ready before you arrive.

Fun is designed here. The coaches who teach our classes all week run the whole session on FIG-certified apparatus, the floor is exclusively yours, and the party room is decorated before you walk in. You bring the cake and the children, and the club looks after the rest.

Coach-led from start to finish

Our coaches run the games, obstacle courses and apparatus time. They teach our classes all week, so the session is safe, structured and pitched to the group, with a skill hidden inside every game.

The floor is yours

Exclusive use of the activity area for the whole session, so your guests have the trampolines, bars, beams and foam pit to themselves.

Decorated before you arrive

Bunting and tableware in your two chosen colours, a Happy Birthday banner, table settings and a decorated party room, all set up ahead of time.

Goody bags and e-invites

Party favours for every guest, customisable e-invitations to send round, and a fridge for the cake.

Bukit Timah has a dedicated birthday party room. See the centre.

One activity per party: gymnastics at any centre, or ninja at Bukit Timah and Dempsey. Coaches pitch the session to the group's age and energy the way they pitch a class, so a party of four-year-olds and a party of twelve-year-olds both get a session built for them.

The group moves into the decorated party room for cake-cutting, the food you have brought along and the goody-bag send-off.

Good to know

Party questions

What happens at a Yard birthday party?

A Yard party runs about two hours: a 90-minute coach-led activity session on the gym floor with games, obstacle courses and real apparatus, followed by 30 minutes of cake-cutting and celebration in a decorated party room. The activity space is exclusively yours during the session.

Can we choose a gymnastics or a ninja party?

Each party runs one activity: gymnastics or ninja. Gymnastics parties are available at all four centres (Jurong, Bukit Timah, Dempsey and Dover). Ninja parties are available at Bukit Timah and Dempsey, the two centres where Ninja Zone runs.

What is included and what should we bring?

Included: themed bunting and tableware in two colours of your choice, a Happy Birthday banner, table settings, goody bags for all guests, customisable e-invitations, a decorated party room and a fridge for your cake. You bring the food and drinks (all parties are self-catered), the birthday cake and any balloons. The Yard is a nut-free zone, so please keep all food nut-free, including in the party room.

How far ahead should we book?

For standard weekend slots, book 2 to 4 weeks ahead. For peak periods such as school holidays and year-end, book 6 to 8 weeks ahead. Weekday and non-standard timings can be requested through the centre team.

Do party guests need a waiver?

Yes. Every child needs a completed liability waiver before stepping onto the gym floor, including party guests. We ask for the final guest list with full names and ages one week before the party so waivers can be arranged in time.

Can we add extra guests to a package?

Each package can take up to two extra children at $80 per child. Beyond that, the next package size up is better value, and the team will advise when you book.

Do we need to be members to book a party?

No. Parties are open to members and non-members alike, and plenty of families meet the club this way. Parties suit children aged roughly 4 to 14 and no gymnastics experience is needed, because coaches pitch the session to the group in front of them. If the birthday child catches the bug, a recreational class trial is the easy next step.
