Good to know

Party FAQs

What happens at a Yard birthday party?

A Yard party runs about 2 hours: a 90-minute coach-led activity session on the gym floor — games, obstacle courses and real apparatus, run by our coaches — followed by 30 minutes of cake-cutting and celebration in a decorated party room. The activity space is exclusively yours during the session.

Can we choose a gymnastics or a ninja party?

Each party runs one activity: gymnastics or ninja. Gymnastics parties are available at all four centres — Jurong, Bukit Timah, Dempsey and Dover. Ninja parties are available at Bukit Timah and Dempsey, the two centres where Ninja Zone runs.

What is included — and what should we bring?

Included: themed bunting and tableware in two colours of your choice, a Happy Birthday banner, table settings, goody bags for all guests, customisable e-invitations, a decorated party room and a fridge for your cake. Not included: food and beverages (all parties are self-catered), the birthday cake (bring your own) and balloons. The Yard is a nut-free zone, so please keep all food nut-free — including in the party room.

How far ahead should we book?

For standard weekend slots, book 2–4 weeks ahead. For peak periods such as school holidays and year-end, book 6–8 weeks ahead. Weekday and non-standard timings can be requested through the centre team.

Do party guests need a waiver?

Yes. Every child needs a completed liability waiver before stepping onto the gym floor, including party guests. We ask for the final guest list with full names and ages one week before the party so waivers can be arranged in time.

Can we add extra guests to a package?

Each package can take up to 2 extra children at $80 per child. Beyond that, the next package size up is better value — the team will advise when you book.

Do we need to be members to book a party?

No — parties are open to members and non-members alike. Parties suit children roughly aged 4–14, and no gymnastics experience is needed; coaches pitch the session to the group on the day. If the birthday child catches the bug, a recreational class trial is the natural next step.
