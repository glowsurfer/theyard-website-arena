The Yard Jurong

The Yard Jurong is a 20,000 sq ft Olympic-grade gymnastics arena at Perennial Business City (opened November 2025) — recreational classes from 18 months, Tumbling & Trampoline, an Adult Open Session, and the High Performance hub for The Yard's competitive WAG/MAG pathway.

Good to know

Jurong FAQs

Which classes run at Jurong?

Jurong runs recreational gymnastics — Gym Tots (18 months–3), KinderTots (3–4) and FUNdamentals (5–10) — plus Tumbling & Trampoline (10–16, only at Jurong) and a 90-minute Adult Open Session (16+). Jurong is also the High Performance hub for the competitive WAG/MAG pathway, with squads entering by free assessment and invitation.

Does Jurong run Ninja Zone or Warriors?

No. Ninja Zone runs at Bukit Timah and Dempsey, and Warriors runs at Bukit Timah and Dover. The Jurong team can help you choose between the programs here or point you to the nearest centre for the rest.

Where is The Yard Jurong and how do I contact it?

1 Venture Avenue, #03-03A, Perennial Business City, Singapore 608521. Phone +65 6816 8022, WhatsApp +65 8749 3709, email enquiries@theyard.com.sg.

What events are hosted at the Jurong arena?

The Jurong arena hosts The Yard's flagship meets — the Lion City Classic (sanctioned, April) and the Diamond Classic (invitational, March) — on a FIG-compliant spring floor. See events →
