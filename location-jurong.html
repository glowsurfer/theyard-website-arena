The Yard Jurong

The Yard Jurong is the High-Performance Arena of the club: 17,500 square feet of Olympic-grade apparatus at 1 Venture Avenue, Perennial Business City, a five-minute sheltered walk from Jurong East MRT. Toddlers take their first Gym Tots class on the same floor the Jurong squads train on, teens take Tumbling and Trampoline here and at no other Yard centre, adults have an Open Session, and every March and April the Diamond Classic and the Lion City Classic bring the club's competitive calendar home.

Arena
The facility

An Olympic-grade arena,
out west.

Jurong opened in November 2025 as the newest and largest Yard centre, built to international competition standard. Squads, toddlers, teens and adults share it every day, and one coaching philosophy runs through all of them: foundations before sophistication, and a coach who decides every level move.

17,500 square feet

The largest Yard centre, with full WAG and MAG apparatus under one roof and room for several classes to run at once, which keeps ratios small and coaches close.

FIG-compliant spring floor

The same competition surface the meets are judged on, so a FUNdamentals cartwheel and a Level 10 tumbling pass share one floor.

Foam pits and Olympic trampolines

Multiple foam pits and Olympic trampolines let children attempt a new skill with a soft landing. That is where courage gets built, one rep at a time.

Host arena

Home of the Lion City Classic every April and the Diamond Classic every March, with a parent viewing area and a nut-free policy throughout.

Good to know

Jurong questions

Which classes run at Jurong?

Jurong runs recreational gymnastics (Gym Tots for 18 months to 3, KinderTots for 3 to 4 and FUNdamentals for 5 to 10), Tumbling and Trampoline for 10 to 16 year olds, which runs only at Jurong, and a 90-minute Adult Open Session for ages 16 and over. WAG and MAG squads train here, as they do at every Yard centre, the club's meets are hosted here, and a place in a squad begins with a free assessment and an invitation.

Does Jurong run Ninja Zone or Warriors?

No. Ninja Zone runs at Bukit Timah and Dempsey, and Warriors runs at Bukit Timah and Dover. Levels and coaching are the same across the club, so the Jurong team can help you choose between the programmes here or point you to the nearest centre for the rest.

How do I get to Jurong and where do I park?

The Yard Jurong is at 1 Venture Avenue, #03-03A, Perennial Business City, Singapore 608521, a five-minute sheltered walk from Jurong East MRT. Parking at Perennial Business City is paid: $1.63 for the first hour, then $0.82 per 30 minutes, with a flat $2.73 after 5pm and a 15-minute grace period.

What events are hosted at the Jurong arena?

The Jurong arena hosts The Yard's flagship meets, the Lion City Classic (sanctioned, April) and the Diamond Classic (invitational, March), on a FIG-compliant spring floor. See the events calendar.
