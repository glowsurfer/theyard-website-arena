Competition day at The Yard.

The Yard's competition calendar runs all year: the Diamond Classic in March and the sanctioned Lion City Classic in April at our 17,500 square foot Jurong arena, invitationals hosted for other clubs, and every December the whole club, all four centres and every programme, takes the floor together at the Christmas Show. Spectators are welcome, and entry at Yard-hosted meets is $5 at the door.

Everything a spectator
needs to know.

Yard-hosted meets run at the Jurong arena, 17,500 square feet with an FIG-compliant spring floor, and member families from all four centres fill most of the seats. Each event announcement confirms the details for that meet. Plan your visit to Jurong.

$5 per person

Spectator entry at Yard-hosted meets is $5, paid at venue check-in.

Cash or PayNow

Payment is taken at the door only. There are no advance ticket sales for spectator seating.

First come, first seated

Roughly 200 seats per session on a first come, first served basis, so arrive early for finals sessions.

Keep the floor clean

Please keep food and drink out of the seating area. Every Yard venue is nut-free.

Filming and photography stay off limits in viewing areas for safeguarding reasons, and exceptions are sometimes announced at hosted showcases. Each event's announcement confirms the details.

Good to know

Event questions

Who competes at The Yard's competitions?

Competitions such as the Lion City Classic and Diamond Classic are for competitive squad (WAG and MAG) gymnasts. Recreational children have their own moments to shine: Skills Matrix assessments with medals three times a year, and the all-centres Christmas Show every December.

Can I come and watch a Yard-hosted competition?

Yes, spectators are welcome at Yard-hosted meets. Entry is $5 per person, payable in cash or PayNow at venue check-in, with no advance sales. Seating is first come, first served with roughly 200 seats per session, and no food or drink is allowed in the seating area. Each event announcement confirms the details for that meet.

How does a club enter the Lion City Classic?

The Lion City Classic is open to clubs across Singapore, with WAG Levels 2 to 10 and MAG Foundation to Level 5 judged to SGLP and USAG standards. Clubs can request the entry pack by emailing enquiries@theyard.com.sg.

Can I film or take photos at events?

For the safeguarding of all children, filming and photography are off limits in viewing areas. Exceptions are sometimes announced at hosted showcases and competitions, and the event briefing confirms what is allowed.

Where can I find results and highlights from past events?

Results, photos and highlights are posted on The Yard's Instagram and Facebook, usually within days of each event. Follow @theyardgymnastics on Instagram for results first.

How does my child get to compete one day?

Through the competitive pathway. Children who progress well in recreational classes can be put forward for a free assessment, and suitable gymnasts are invited into a WAG or MAG squad. Squad members compete at the Diamond Classic, Lion City Classic and other meets, with international tours on the squad calendar. How the pathway works.
