Competitions & Shows

The Yard hosts a year-round calendar of gymnastics competitions and showcases in Singapore — headlined by the Lion City Classic (April) and Diamond Classic (March) at our 20,000 sq ft Jurong arena, and an all-centres Christmas Show every December. Spectator entry at Yard-hosted meets is S$5 at the door.

Good to know

Event FAQs

Who competes at The Yard's competitions?

Competitions like the Lion City Classic and Diamond Classic are for competitive squad (WAG/MAG) gymnasts — recreational students don't compete at these meets. Recreational children have their own moments to shine: Skills Matrix assessments and medals three times a year, and the all-centres Christmas Show every December.

Can I come and watch a Yard-hosted competition?

Yes — spectators are welcome at Yard-hosted meets. Entry is S$5 per person, payable in cash or PayNow at venue check-in on the day (no advance sales). Seating is first-come, first-served with roughly 200 seats per session, and no food or drink is allowed in the seating area. Details are confirmed in each event's announcement.

How does a club enter the Lion City Classic?

The Lion City Classic is open to clubs across Singapore, with WAG Levels 2–10 and MAG Foundation–Level 5 judged to SGLP and USAG standards. Clubs can request the entry pack by emailing enquiries@theyard.com.sg.

Can I film or take photos at events?

For the safeguarding of all children, filming and photography are not permitted in viewing areas. Exceptions are sometimes announced at hosted showcases and competitions — the event briefing on the day will confirm what's allowed.

Where can I find results and highlights from past events?

Results, photos and highlights are posted on The Yard's Instagram and Facebook — usually within days of each event. Follow @theyardgymnastics on Instagram for results first.

How does my child get to compete one day?

Through the competitive pathway. Children who progress well in recreational classes can be put forward for a free assessment, and suitable gymnasts are invited into a WAG or MAG squad. Squad members compete at the Diamond Classic, Lion City Classic and other meets, with international tours on the squad calendar. How the pathway works →
