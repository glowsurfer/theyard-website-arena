Why it works

Most team events ask people to sit, watch or compete at trivia. A Yard event asks them to move — and adults on a spring floor stop performing their job titles very quickly. Real coaches keep it safe and structured; the apparatus does the rest.

  • Coach-led from warm-up to finish — nobody is left to wing it
  • Scales from small teams to large groups; every fitness level joins in
  • FIG-certified apparatus, foam pits and trampolines at every centre
  • Venue-only hire available if you're bringing your own program

Good to know

Corporate FAQs

What corporate events can The Yard host?

Three formats: team-building activities, brand activations and private gymnastics experiences — plus venue hire of the gym space itself. Every event is coach-led and shaped around your group, and all four Singapore centres (Jurong, Bukit Timah, Dempsey and Dover) are available.

Which companies have held events at The Yard?

Past corporate clients include LinkedIn, TP ICAP and Chanel. See partners & clients →

Do participants need gymnastics experience or a certain fitness level?

No. Sessions are run by The Yard's coaches and scaled to the group — the point is safe, structured, genuinely fun movement for adults of every fitness level, not gymnastics ability.

How much does a corporate event cost?

Events are quoted individually based on centre, group size, format and timing. Email enquiries@theyard.com.sg with your dates and headcount and the team will come back with a proposal.

Can we bring food and drinks?

Food arrangements are agreed as part of your event plan — note that every Yard centre is a strictly nut-free zone. Anyone taking part on the gym floor completes The Yard's standard liability waiver first.
