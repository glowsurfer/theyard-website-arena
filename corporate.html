What we host

Three formats on
one gym floor.

Every event is coach-led and built around your group. Tell the team the occasion and the headcount, and the session is shaped from there, the way a coach plans a class around the children in front of them.

For teams

Team building

Swap the escape room for foam pits, trampolines and Olympic-grade apparatus. Coaches run structured challenges that get everyone moving and laughing, whatever their fitness level.

For brands

Brand activations

Launches and activations in a venue people remember, from the 17,500 square foot Jurong arena to Dempsey's heritage building with free parking.

For groups

Private gymnastics experiences

A private coach-led session for your group: flips into foam pits, first handstands and trampoline time. Structured play for adults, done safely.

Why it works

Most team events ask people to sit, watch or answer trivia. A Yard event asks them to move, and adults on a spring floor stop performing their job titles within minutes. Fun is designed into the session the way it is designed into every class here, and the coaches keep it safe and structured while the apparatus does the rest.

  • Coach-led from warm-up to finish, so nobody is left to wing it
  • Scales from small teams to large groups, and every fitness level joins in
  • FIG-certified apparatus, foam pits and trampolines at every centre
  • Venue-only hire is available if you are bringing your own programme

Good to know

Corporate questions

What corporate events can The Yard host?

The Yard hosts team-building activities, brand activations and private gymnastics experiences, plus venue hire of the gym space itself. Every event is coach-led and shaped around your group, and all four Singapore centres (Jurong, Bukit Timah, Dempsey and Dover) are available.

Which companies have held events at The Yard?

Past corporate clients include LinkedIn, TP ICAP and Chanel. See partners and clients.

Do participants need gymnastics experience or a certain fitness level?

No. Sessions are run by The Yard's coaches and scaled to the group. The aim is safe, structured and genuinely fun movement for adults of every fitness level.

How much does a corporate event cost?

Events are quoted individually on centre, group size, format and timing. Email enquiries@theyard.com.sg with your dates and headcount and the team will come back with a proposal.

Can we bring food and drinks?

Food arrangements are agreed as part of your event plan. Every Yard centre is a strictly nut-free zone, and anyone taking part on the gym floor completes The Yard's standard liability waiver first.

Plan it with the team

Book the floor.

Tell us your preferred centre, rough headcount and dates, and the team will come back with a format and a quote. LinkedIn, TP ICAP and Chanel have done it, and your team can be next.
