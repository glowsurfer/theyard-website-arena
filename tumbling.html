Tumbling & Trampoline

Tumbling & Trampoline is The Yard's floor, vault and tumble-track program for ages 10–16, offered at the Jurong centre only — three levels progress from rolls, cartwheels and round offs to back handsprings, aerials and twisting somersaults, scored on the program's own Bronze, Silver and Gold medal scale.

Why it works

The confidence sport
for tweens & teens

Trampoline is its own track — a child can train it instead of, or alongside, artistic gymnastics. It's also one of the best entry points for a 10–16 year old starting from scratch.

Skills that look spectacular

Somersaults, aerials and walkovers — the kind of skills teens actually want to show off, built step by step on floor, vault and tumble track.

Strength to back it up

Many athletes report feeling fitter, stronger and more grounded in their body within a single term — real conditioning behind every trick.

A clear 10-skill ladder

Every level has a 10-skill matrix scored on Tumbling's own Bronze, Silver and Gold scale — progress is visible, and levels are earned, not time-served.

How progress works

Tumbling's own
medal scale

Tumbling is assessed within The Yard's Skills Matrix assessments but scored on its own points scale — 10 skills per level, each contributing to a Bronze, Silver or Gold result.

01

10 skills per level

Beginner, Intermediate and Advanced each carry a 10-skill matrix across floor, vault and tumble track.

02

Bronze · 10–14 pts

Skills performed with assistance or developing technique — the foundation band on the scoring scale.

03

Silver · 15–25 pts

Greater independence and cleaner shapes — most of the matrix performed with confidence.

04

Gold · 26–30 pts

Unaided skills with correct technique across the matrix — the signal a coach looks for before moving an athlete up.

Good to know

Tumbling FAQs

Which centre runs Tumbling & Trampoline?

Tumbling & Trampoline runs at The Yard's Jurong centre only — the same centre as the High Performance competitive hub. It is not offered at Bukit Timah, Dempsey or Dover.

What ages is Tumbling & Trampoline for?

Ages 10–16. Beginner and Intermediate classes run 1 hour; Advanced runs 1.5 hours. It's designed as a confidence sport for tweens and teens — including complete beginners and older starters.

Does my teen need gymnastics experience to start?

No — Beginner starts from rebound jumps, handstand pops, cartwheels and bridge work. It's one of the best entry points at The Yard for a 10–16 year old starting from scratch.

How is progress measured in Tumbling?

Each level has a 10-skill matrix, and Tumbling uses its own scoring scale within Skills Matrix assessments: Bronze (10–14 points), Silver (15–25) and Gold (26–30). Coaches move athletes up when the skills are mastered.

Can my child do Tumbling alongside artistic gymnastics?

Yes — Trampoline is positioned as its own track, so a child can train Tumbling & Trampoline instead of or alongside artistic gymnastics. It works well as a co-track for older recreational gymnasts.
