Ninja Zone

Ninja Zone is The Yard's obstacle-and-agility program, blending gymnastics, parkour, obstacle courses and martial-arts elements for ages 3–10 — at Bukit Timah and Dempsey only. Open entry starts at Ninja Tots (3–4) and Ninja White (5–10); Yellow and Green levels are by coach invitation.

Why it works

Sport that feels
like play

Sessions rotate between gymnastics, parkour, obstacle-course work and martial-arts elements — a different mix every class, so children never plateau on one style of movement.

Channels big energy

High-energy children get "Ninja Warrior on TV" energy in a coached, safe setting — jumping, flipping and conquering obstacles instead of bouncing off the walls at home.

Strength, agility, speed

Wall runs, vaults, climbs and rig obstacles build whole-body athleticism fast — because they genuinely want to be there.

A try-anything mindset

Every obstacle is a small win. Confidence and coordination compound session by session — with skill matrices and invitation levels giving children a ladder to climb.

Ninja White

5 – 10 years1 hourOpen entry — the core ninja class

The core Ninja Zone class: gymnastics, parkour, obstacle-course work and martial-arts elements, rotated each session. It builds whole-body athleticism and a try-anything mindset in children who want more action than a classic gym class.

Ninja Yellow & Ninja Green

1.5 hoursBy coach assessment & invitation

The step-up levels, reached by invitation rather than open booking:

  • Ninja Yellow — a 25-skill matrix: kong vault, back handspring prep, wall run flip, and suspended and hanging rope work on the rig.
  • Ninja Green — elite-level ninja skill, entered after Yellow: back handspring, front tuck, wall spin, double kong and complex obstacle sequences.

Coaches assess children in Ninja White and invite those who are ready — the centre team can tell you where your child fits and confirm current session availability.

Good to know

Ninja Zone FAQs

Which centres run Ninja Zone?

Ninja Zone runs at two Yard centres: Bukit Timah and Dempsey. It is not offered at Jurong or Dover. Bukit Timah has a custom-built ninja rig with obstacles like bungee cords, cargo net and ninja rings.

What age can my child start Ninja Zone?

Ninja Tots takes children from age 3, and Ninja White takes ages 5–10. Both are open entry levels with no experience needed — Ninja Yellow and Ninja Green come later, by coach invitation only.

Is Ninja Zone the same as gymnastics?

No — Ninja Zone blends gymnastics with parkour, obstacle-course training and martial-arts elements, rotated each session. It suits high-energy children who want "Ninja Warrior on TV" energy; children who prefer classic apparatus work may enjoy recreational gymnastics, which runs at all four centres.

How do the Ninja Yellow and Green levels work?

Ninja Yellow and Ninja Green are 1.5-hour invitation-only levels. Coaches assess children in Ninja White and invite those who are ready — Yellow adds a 25-skill matrix with kong vaults, back handspring prep, wall run flips and suspended rope rig work, and Green builds to back handsprings, front tucks, wall spins and double kongs. Ask the team at your centre about where your child fits.

Are there Ninja Zone holiday camps?

Yes — Ninja Zone camps run at Bukit Timah and Dempsey during the school holidays, alongside The Yard's gymnastics camps. See holiday camps →
