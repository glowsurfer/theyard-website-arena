Ninja Zone

Ninja Zone is gymnastics meets adventure: obstacle courses, parkour, acrobatics and martial-arts moves, coached for children aged 3 to 10 at Bukit Timah, our Ninja Hub, and at Dempsey. Ninja Tots (3 to 4) and Ninja White (5 to 10) are open to every child, and Ninja Yellow and Ninja Green come later, when your child's coach invites them up.

Play · Fueled with fun, designed for progress

The sport that feels
like play.

Every session rotates through gymnastics, parkour, obstacle-course work and martial-arts moves, so the mix changes each week and the strength builds without anyone noticing the conditioning. Children come because it looks like the ninja shows on television, and they stay because the coach keeps adding one obstacle they could just about manage last week and can own this week.

Big energy, well spent

A child who bounces off the walls at home gets a coached, safe place to jump, climb, swing and beat obstacles, with one coach to every eight children, and comes home calmer for it.

Strength, agility and speed

Wall runs, vaults, climbs and rig obstacles build whole-body athleticism quickly, because the training feels like the game they wanted to play anyway. Fun is designed into every session, and so is the conditioning underneath it.

Small wins, compounded

Every obstacle is a small win, and small steps compounded are how The Yard coaches everything. The 21-skill matrix gives your child a ladder they can see, and their coach decides when they are ready for the next rung.

The levels

From first obstacle
to elite ninja.

Two open-entry levels get every child started, and two invitation levels reward the ones who fall in love with it. Your child's coach calls every move up.

Ninja Tots

3 to 4 years1 hourOpen entry, no experience needed

Ninja training at toddler scale. Obstacle courses, climbing and movement games turn boundless energy into focused movement, coordination and the listening skills that carry straight into pre-school. Progress is the promise here, and the pace belongs to your child.

What parents notice

A toddler who leaves class steadier on their feet, prouder of what they climbed and crossed, and a little readier to follow an instruction the first time it is given.

Ninja White

5 to 10 years1 hourOpen entry, the core ninja class

The core Ninja Zone class, and the one most members stay in for years. Gymnastics, parkour, obstacle-course work and martial-arts moves rotate through each session, building whole-body athleticism and a try-anything mindset in children who want more action than a classic gym class gives them.

Skills and assessment

Ninja White follows a 21-skill matrix: handstand, ninja roll, palm spin, tic tac, wall run and lache on the floor and vault, chin-ups and under-bars on the bars, cat crawls and vaulting on the beam, and bungee cords, cargo net and ninja rings on the rig. The custom-built rig at Bukit Timah, our Ninja Hub, is the signature playground, and coaches keep every child at the edge of what they can do with the strain kept out.

Ninja Yellow & Ninja Green

1.5 hoursBy coach assessment and invitation

The step-up levels, reached by invitation once a coach sees a child ready for more. Every step earns its place, so nobody is hurried up the ladder before the strength and control are there.

  • Ninja Yellow runs a 25-skill matrix: kong vault, back handspring preparation, wall run flip, handstand walks, and suspended and hanging rope work on the rig.
  • Ninja Green is the elite level, entered after Yellow: back handspring, front tuck, wall spin, double kong and complex obstacle sequences.

Coaches assess children in Ninja White and invite those who are ready. Ask the centre team where your child fits and which sessions currently run.

Yellow and Green are reached by coach invitation. Ask the Bukit Timah team or the Dempsey team about your child's next step.

Good to know

Ninja Zone questions

Which centres run Ninja Zone?

Ninja Zone runs at two Yard centres: Bukit Timah, our Ninja Hub, with a custom-built rig of bungee cords, cargo net and ninja rings, and Dempsey, the Scenic Studio on Dempsey Hill. Jurong and Dover run gymnastics programmes instead.

What age can my child start Ninja Zone?

Ninja Tots takes children from age 3 and Ninja White takes ages 5 to 10. Both are open-entry levels with no experience needed. Ninja Yellow and Ninja Green come later, by coach invitation.

Is Ninja Zone the same as gymnastics?

Ninja Zone is gymnastics meets adventure. It blends gymnastics with parkour, obstacle-course training and martial-arts moves, rotated each session, and it suits high-energy children who want the feel of a ninja show. Children who prefer classic apparatus work usually love recreational gymnastics, which runs at all four centres, and plenty of members do both.

How do the Ninja Yellow and Green levels work?

Ninja Yellow and Ninja Green are 90-minute invitation levels. Coaches assess children in Ninja White and invite those who are ready. Yellow adds a 25-skill matrix with kong vaults, back handspring preparation, wall run flips and rope work on the rig, and Green builds to back handsprings, front tucks, wall spins and double kongs. Ask the team at your centre about where your child fits.

Are there Ninja Zone holiday camps?

Yes. Ninja Zone camps run at Bukit Timah and Dempsey during the school holidays, alongside The Yard's gymnastics camps. See holiday camps.

Ready when they are

Try a ninja class.

Ninja Zone runs at Bukit Timah and Dempsey. Pick your centre and book straight into a class, and your child's coach will place them at Tots or White in the first session. The trial fee is shown at booking, and no experience is needed.
