Good to know

Freestyle FAQs

Where does Freestyle run?

Freestyle is based at The Yard's Dover centre (Singapore Polytechnic Graduates' Guild). Freestyle holiday camps run at Dover every school holiday; for current weekly class options, enquire with the Dover team on WhatsApp.

What is Freestyle gymnastics?

Freestyle combines traditional gymnastics and acrobatic stunts with freedom of movement. Children develop spatial awareness, strength, agility and flow alongside personal expression and creativity — building athletic skill through self-expression rather than rigid routine.

What age can my child start Freestyle?

Freestyle is for ages 7 and up. Freestyle Foundation is the 1-hour entry level; Freestyle Intermediate is a 1.5-hour class with bigger movement and more complex sequences, and older groupings run for ages 12+.

How is Freestyle different from recreational gymnastics?

Recreational gymnastics follows a structured apparatus rotation and a 20-skill Skills Matrix per level. Freestyle keeps the athletic foundations but gives children creative ownership — acrobatic stunts, flow and personal style matter as much as technique. Children who want a structured medal pathway usually pick recreational; children who want to move their way pick Freestyle.

How do I book a Freestyle trial?

Contact the Dover team — Freestyle camp places are bookable each school holiday, and the team will confirm current weekly class availability and trial arrangements for Foundation and Intermediate. WhatsApp Dover →
