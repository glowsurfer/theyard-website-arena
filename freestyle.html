Freestyle gymnastics

Freestyle blends gymnastics and acrobatic stunts with freedom of movement, for children aged 7 and up, at Dover, our All-Rounder centre at SPGG. Freestyle holiday camps run at Dover every school holiday, and the Dover team can tell you which weekly Foundation and Intermediate sessions are running this term.

Belong · Individual journeys, unique pathways, one community

For children who want
to move their own way.

Spatial awareness, strength, agility and flow develop alongside personal expression and creativity. Every child is on their own path here, and the coach builds the next step around them inside a structure that keeps it safe, which is how you end up with an athlete who genuinely loves what they do.

Creative ownership

Children choose how to link stunts, transitions and tricks. Style and flow matter as much as technique, and that ownership is why they keep coming back.

Real gymnastics underneath

Under the flair sit genuine gymnastics and acrobatics: spatial awareness, strength and agility, trained by qualified Yard coaches on FIG-certified apparatus.

Athletes who love it

When training feels like self-expression, children show up wanting more. Freestyle members move with confidence and would happily do it all day, and the ones who later leave for another sport take that confidence with them.

Freestyle Foundation

Ages 7 and up1 hourEntry level, no experience needed

The entry point: foundational movements with acrobatic flair. Children learn to roll, vault, balance and land safely, then start adding the transitions and tricks that make freestyle theirs.

What a session looks like

Warm-up and conditioning, then guided exploration: foundational gymnastics shapes, acrobatic basics and free-flowing movement lines, coached for safety and quality throughout.

Good to know

Freestyle questions

Where does Freestyle run?

Freestyle is based at The Yard's Dover centre, our All-Rounder at the Singapore Polytechnic Graduates' Guild. Freestyle holiday camps run at Dover every school holiday, and the Dover team confirms weekly class options on WhatsApp.

What is Freestyle gymnastics?

Freestyle combines traditional gymnastics and acrobatic stunts with freedom of movement. Children develop spatial awareness, strength, agility and flow alongside personal expression and creativity, building athletic skill through self-expression.

What age can my child start Freestyle?

Freestyle is for ages 7 and up. Freestyle Foundation is the one-hour entry level, and Freestyle Intermediate is a ninety-minute class with bigger movement and more complex sequences. Older groups run from age 12.

How is Freestyle different from recreational gymnastics?

Recreational gymnastics follows a structured apparatus rotation and a 20-skill Skills Matrix per level. Freestyle keeps the athletic foundations and gives children creative ownership, where acrobatic stunts, flow and personal style matter as much as technique. Children who want a structured medal pathway usually pick recreational, and children who want to move their own way pick Freestyle.

How do I book a Freestyle trial?

Freestyle camp places are bookable through the Dover booking portal each school holiday. For weekly Foundation and Intermediate classes, WhatsApp the Dover team and they will confirm current availability and trial arrangements.
