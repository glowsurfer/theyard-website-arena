The Yard Dover

The Yard Dover is the All-Rounder of the club and our original home: a full apparatus hall at SPGG, 1010 Dover Road, where the recreational pathway runs longest. A child can start in Gym Tots at 18 months and stay coached through FUNdamentals, Warriors and Senior Warriors right up to 16, boys have their own classes on the boys' apparatus, and the Freestyle holiday camps are based here.

Holiday camps at Dover

Five mornings, three hours each, in every school holiday. Gymnastics camps run for KinderTots and FUNdamentals ages, Freestyle camps bring flips, tricking and floor movement to children aged 7 and up, and the coaches and the care are the same as in term.

The facility

Where the pathway
runs longest.

At SPGG on Dover Road, a recreational gymnast can keep progressing from a first Gym Tots class right through their teens on a full hall of competition-grade apparatus. The coach calls every level move when the skills are there, and every step earns its place.

A pathway to 16

The Yard centre with Senior Warriors, so a child who starts at 18 months has a coached level to grow into all the way to 16, with medals to show for each one.

Boys' classes

Dedicated Boys Tots and Boys FUNdamentals classes with parallel bars, rings and pommel work, alongside the mixed recreational timetable.

Freestyle camps

Dover is the home of The Yard's Freestyle camps: flips, tricking and floor movement for children who want to move their own way.

Viewing area

Watch every class from the parent viewing area, on FIG-certified apparatus. The centre is nut-free throughout, and the coach is on the floor before the class starts.

Good to know

Dover questions

Which classes run at Dover?

Dover runs recreational gymnastics (Gym Tots for 18 months to 3, KinderTots for 3 to 4 and FUNdamentals for 5 to 10), Warriors (5 to 10) and Senior Warriors (10 to 16), dedicated boys' classes (Boys Tots and Boys FUNdamentals), and Freestyle holiday camps. Dover is the club's All-Rounder and its original home, and the only Yard centre with Senior Warriors.

Does Dover run classes for older kids and teens?

Yes. Dover has the longest recreational pathway of any Yard centre: Warriors (5 to 10) progresses into Senior Warriors (10 to 16), which runs at Dover. Teens aged 10 to 16 can also consider Tumbling and Trampoline at Jurong.

Does Dover run Freestyle classes?

Dover runs Freestyle holiday camps, with floor-based flips, tricking and creative movement. Weekly Freestyle classes come and go with the timetable, so ask the Dover team for the latest. See Freestyle.

How do I get to Dover and where do I park?

The Yard Dover is at 1010 Dover Road, #01-01, SPGG, Singapore 139658. Parking at SPGG is paid, and SPGG members get two hours free.

Book a trial at Dover for any recreational class, Warriors, Senior Warriors or a boys' class, and the fee is shown at booking. If you would rather talk it through first, the Dover team is a WhatsApp away and will recommend one class, the one that fits.
