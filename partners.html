Why partner

What you are
plugging into.

Every partnership runs on the same club our members train with: the same venues, the same coaches, the same apparatus and the same four pillars, move, play, grow and belong.

Four real venues

Jurong, the High-Performance Arena at 17,500 square feet; Bukit Timah, the Ninja Hub at KAP Mall; Dempsey, the Scenic Studio in a heritage building with free parking; and Dover, the All-Rounder at SPGG.

Coach-led everything

Corporate sessions, school visits and events are run by The Yard's own coaching team on FIG-certified apparatus.

A community that turns up

Hosted meets and shows, including the Lion City Classic, the Diamond Classic and the annual Christmas Show, fill the Jurong arena with members and their families, and sponsorship packages are quoted on request.

Good to know

Partner questions

Which companies have worked with The Yard?

LinkedIn, TP ICAP and Chanel have held corporate events at The Yard. Events run at all four centres and are quoted on enquiry. How corporate events work.

Which schools does The Yard partner with?

The Yard works with schools across Singapore and publishes a partner's name only with that school's agreement. The roster is being confirmed and will appear on this page. Schools can start a conversation at enquiries@theyard.com.sg.

Can we sponsor a Yard event?

Yes. Sponsorship packages cover in-gym digital screens, event partnerships and competition naming rights at our hosted meets and shows, and they are quoted on request. See the events.
