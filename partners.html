03 — Why partner

What you're
plugging into

Every partnership runs on the same club our members train with — no rented kit, no borrowed coaches.

Four real venues

Jurong's 20,000 sq ft Olympic-grade arena, Bukit Timah at KAP Mall, Dempsey's heritage setting with free parking, and Dover at SPGG.

Coach-led everything

Corporate sessions, school visits and events are run by The Yard's own coaching team on FIG-certified apparatus.

An events stage

Hosted meets and shows — Lion City Classic, Diamond Classic, the annual Christmas Show — with sponsorship packages quoted on request.
