Programmes

Two programmes carry
the whole club.

Everything else on the floor, from Ninja Zone to tumbling to the adult sessions, hangs off those two, and the same coaching philosophy runs through all of it.

Trial
Your first visit

A trial takes one hour
and three easy steps.

Most children arrive a little unsure and leave asking when they can come back. This is what happens, so nobody has to guess, and a trial commits you to nothing.

Step one

Book a trial at your centre

Pick a class online in about two minutes. The trial fee is shown at booking, no experience is needed, and the team will move the date if something comes up.

Step two

Meet the coach and watch it happen

Arrive ten minutes early in fitted sportswear, hair tied back, bare feet. The coach greets your child by name, the class runs warm-up, apparatus rotations and cool-down, and you watch every minute from the viewing area.

Step three

Start the pathway

If they loved it, the coach recommends a level and your family joins the club as members. Progress is assessed three times a year, with Bronze, Silver and Gold medals to show for it, and the pace belongs to your child.

One pathway, every level.

A toddler's first forward roll and a Level 10 beam routine sit on the same pathway. Good to know

Common questions

What ages does The Yard cater for?

Classes start at 18 months with Gym Tots and run through KinderTots (3 to 4), FUNdamentals (5 to 10), advanced recreational levels and teen programmes such as Senior Warriors (10 to 16, Dover) and Tumbling and Trampoline (10 to 16, Jurong). Adults aged 16 and over train at Jurong (Open Session) and Bukit Timah (Adult Foundation Gymnastics).

Which programmes run at which centre?

Recreational gymnastics (Gym Tots, KinderTots, FUNdamentals) runs at all four centres. Warriors runs at Bukit Timah and Dover; Senior Warriors at Dover; boys' classes at Dempsey and Dover. Ninja Zone runs at Bukit Timah and Dempsey. Tumbling and Trampoline runs at Jurong. Adult sessions run at Jurong and Bukit Timah. The competitive WAG and MAG squads train at all four centres, and the Lion City Classic and Diamond Classic are hosted at Jurong.

How do I book a trial class?

Book a trial for any recreational class through your centre's online booking page, or WhatsApp the centre team and they will find the right class and time. The trial fee is shown at booking. Competitive squads start with a free assessment arranged with the centre team.

Does my child need any experience to start?

No. Every recreational programme starts from beginner level and children can join at any time of year, with fees pro-rated for the remaining classes in the term. Coaches place each child at the right level after the first class.

Where are your centres located?

Four centres across Singapore: Jurong (1 Venture Avenue, #03-03A, Perennial Business City, 608521), Bukit Timah (9 King Albert Park, #B1-01, KAP Mall, 598332), Dempsey (72 Loewen Road, Dempsey Hill, 248828) and Dover (1010 Dover Road, #01-01, SPGG, 139658).

How does the competitive pathway work?

Entry to our competitive Women's and Men's Artistic Gymnastics squads is by assessment and invitation, and the assessment is free. Squads train 6 to 24 hours per week on SGLP and USAG standards over an August to July season, at all four centres, and the club's meets are hosted at the Jurong arena. Read how selection works.

Do you host birthday parties, camps and events?

Yes. Birthday parties run at all four centres: a 90-minute coach-led session plus 30 minutes in a decorated party room. Holiday camps run in every school-holiday window (March, June, September, December). We also host the Lion City Classic and Diamond Classic at Jurong and an annual Christmas Show. See Parties, Camps and Events.
