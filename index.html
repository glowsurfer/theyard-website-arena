10 — Good to know

Questions, answered

What ages does The Yard cater for?

Classes start at 18 months with Gym Tots and run through KinderTots (3–4), FUNdamentals (5–10), advanced recreational levels and teen programs such as Senior Warriors (10–16, Dover) and Tumbling & Trampoline (10–16, Jurong). Adults 16+ train at Jurong (Open Session) and Bukit Timah (Adult Foundation Gymnastics).

Which programs run at which centre?

Recreational gymnastics (Gym Tots, KinderTots, FUNdamentals) runs at all four centres. Warriors runs at Bukit Timah and Dover; Senior Warriors and boys' classes at Dover, with boys' classes also at Dempsey. Ninja Zone runs at Bukit Timah and Dempsey only. Tumbling & Trampoline runs at Jurong only. Adult sessions run at Jurong and Bukit Timah. The competitive WAG/MAG pathway is based at the Jurong High Performance hub.

How do I book a trial class?

Book a trial for any recreational class through the centre's booking portal, or WhatsApp the centre team and they'll find the right class and time. Competitive squads start with a free assessment arranged with the centre team instead of a trial.

Where are your centres located?

Four centres across Singapore: Jurong (1 Venture Avenue, #03-03A, Perennial Business City, 608521), Bukit Timah (9 King Albert Park, #B1-01, KAP Mall, 598332), Dempsey (72 Loewen Road, Dempsey Hill, 248828) and Dover (1010 Dover Road, #01-01, SPGG, 139658).

How does the competitive pathway work?

Entry to our competitive Women's and Men's Artistic Gymnastics squads is by assessment and invitation, not open enrolment — and the assessment is free. Squads train 6–24 hours per week on SGLP and USAG standards over an August–July season, with the High Performance hub at Jurong. Read how selection works →

Do you host birthday parties, camps and events?

Yes. Birthday parties run at all four centres — a 90-minute coach-led session plus 30 minutes in a decorated party room. Holiday camps run in every school-holiday window (March, June, September, December). We also host the Lion City Classic and Diamond Classic at Jurong and an annual Christmas Show. See Parties, Camps and Events.
