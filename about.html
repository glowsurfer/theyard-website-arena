Our Story

The Yard is a Singapore gymnastics club founded in 2016 by international coach Rosanna Trigg, with four centres — Jurong, Bukit Timah, Dempsey and Dover — and one philosophy: progress is the point, not pace.

The founder

Thirty years
on the floor.

The Yard was founded by Rosanna Trigg — an internationally qualified coach with more than 30 years of experience as a competitive gymnast and coach across the UK, Mexico and Singapore.

Rosanna founded her first club, Gym With Me, in 2010, and established The Yard in 2016. Since then the club has grown into four centres across Singapore — most recently the 20,000 sq ft Olympic-grade arena at Jurong — while keeping the same mission: combining high-performance athletic training with holistic child development in a fun, safe and nurturing environment.

Today she still works where she always has: with the athletes, as a competitive specialist for the High Performance squad. Around her is a coaching team drawn from around the world, bringing a global perspective to coaching, planning and curriculums.

Progress
The philosophy

"Progress is the point,
not pace."

Every class, level and assessment at The Yard is built around four convictions about how children actually get good at things.

01

Foundations before sophistication

Skills are layered on solid basics, never rushed. A clean cartwheel today beats a wobbly back handspring tomorrow.

02

Stretched, not strained

Classes live in the zone where children are challenged enough to grow — and supported enough to enjoy it.

03

Plateaus are part of progress

Levels are earned, never time-served. A coach calls every level move when the skills are genuinely there.

04

The exit is part of the design

A successful Yard journey ends with a child who is strong, coordinated and confident — whether or not they stay in gymnastics forever.

What we stand for

Move. Play.
Belong. Grow.

Four words on the wall of every centre — and the test every decision has to pass.

Move

Real physical training on FIG-certified apparatus — strength, balance and coordination that transfer to every sport a child will ever try.

Play

Children work hardest when it feels like play. From Gym Tots to squad conditioning, fun is a method, not a reward.

Belong

An inclusive, nut-free, safeguarding-led club where every child — all ages and abilities — has a place on the floor.

Grow

Confidence, resilience and discipline that show up far beyond the gym — on playgrounds, in classrooms and in other sports.

The team

Coaches from
around the world

The Yard employs gymnastics talent from around the world and Singapore — many coaches have international competitive backgrounds across USAG, FIG and SGLP national programmes, and several are former competitive athletes.

Qualified & current

Every coach holds coaching qualifications appropriate to the apparatus and level taught, current first-aid certification, and ongoing training in safeguarding and duty of care to children.

Rigorously screened

Every coach is interviewed, reference-checked and screened before joining — and The Yard continuously reviews its standards for technical instruction, child safety and class delivery.

Always on the floor

Every class — recreational, competitive, even the self-directed Adult Open Session — is supervised on-floor by a qualified coach.
