The Yard Dempsey

The Yard Dempsey is the Scenic Studio of the club: a restored heritage building surrounded by greenery at 72 Loewen Road on Dempsey Hill, with free parking at the door. Younger families have made it their own. Gym Tots, KinderTots and FUNdamentals run here alongside dedicated boys' classes, Ninja Zone for 3 to 10 year olds and a WAG Kinder Development class on the early competitive pathway, and the bright, airy studio suits a three year old's first class without a parent on the floor.

Dempsey
The facility

A gym with
heritage charm.

Tucked into the greenery of Dempsey Hill, this is the easiest Yard centre to drive to and the one with free parking at the door, which is part of why it is so popular with younger families. The other part is the studio itself: bright, airy and just the right size for a three year old's first class.

Free parking

Park free at Dempsey Hill around the clock and walk straight in. It is the simplest drop-off of any Yard centre.

Heritage setting

A bright, characterful heritage building with cafés and green space all around, and a favourite for corporate events too.

FIG-certified apparatus

The same coaching standards and competition-grade Gymnova and Gao Fei equipment as every Yard centre, so a level earned here is a level everywhere in the club.

Viewing area

Watch every class from the parent viewing area. The centre is nut-free throughout, and the coach is on the floor before the class starts.

Good to know

Dempsey questions

Which classes run at Dempsey?

Dempsey runs recreational gymnastics (Gym Tots for 18 months to 3, KinderTots for 3 to 4 and FUNdamentals for 5 to 10), dedicated boys' classes (Boys Tots and Boys FUNdamentals), Ninja Zone (Ninja Tots for 3 to 4 and Ninja White for 5 to 10), and WAG Kinder Development, an early class on the competitive pathway.

Does Dempsey have parking?

Yes. Dempsey has free parking around the clock at Dempsey Hill, which makes it the easiest Yard centre to drive to and the simplest drop-off for a parent with a toddler on one hip. The centre is at 72 Loewen Road, Singapore 248828.

Does Dempsey run boys' gymnastics classes?

Yes. Dempsey runs dedicated boys' classes, Boys Tots and Boys FUNdamentals, on the boys' apparatus alongside its mixed recreational classes, and Gladiator is the boys' advanced level reached by progression. Dover also runs boys' classes. See the boys' classes.

Where is Dempsey and how do I contact the team?

The Yard Dempsey is at 72 Loewen Road, Dempsey Hill, Singapore 248828. Phone +65 6914 9660, WhatsApp +65 8949 8693, email dempsey@theyard.com.sg.
