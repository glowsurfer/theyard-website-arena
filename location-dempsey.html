Good to know

Dempsey FAQs

Which classes run at Dempsey?

Dempsey runs recreational gymnastics — Gym Tots (18 months–3), KinderTots (3–4) and FUNdamentals (5–10) — plus dedicated boys' classes (Boys Tots and Boys Fundamentals), Ninja Zone (Ninja Tots 3–4 and Ninja White 5–10), and WAG Kinder Development, an early competitive-development class.

Does Dempsey have parking?

Yes — Dempsey is the easiest Yard centre to drive to, with free parking right at Dempsey Hill. The centre is at 72 Loewen Road, Singapore 248828.

Does Dempsey run boys' gymnastics classes?

Yes. Dempsey runs dedicated boys' classes — Boys Tots and Boys Fundamentals — alongside its mixed recreational classes. Dover also runs boys' classes. See boys' classes →

Where is The Yard Dempsey and how do I contact it?

72 Loewen Road, Dempsey Hill, Singapore 248828. Phone +65 6914 9660, WhatsApp +65 8949 8693, email dempsey@theyard.com.sg.
