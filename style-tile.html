12 · Process

How to build a page

  1. Read _spec/ARENA_KIT.md. It holds the page skeleton, the component table, the content rules and the facts that must stay true.
  2. Copy the head boilerplate, header, footer, mobile bar, WhatsApp float and finder dialog verbatim from index.html. Change only the page title, description, canonical, Open Graph and JSON-LD.
  3. Open with a hero--sub, alternate light and dark sections, put a FAQ near the end, close with the gold booking band and three onward links.
  4. Write for the reader as the hero and The Yard as the guide, then run execution/check_writing_style.py until it reports zero errors.
  5. Render the page in a browser at desktop and phone widths before calling it done.
