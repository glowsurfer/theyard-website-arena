Why The Yard

We won't hand you a glossy brochure of claims. Here's the real foundation a school program would stand on.

A decade of kids' coaching

Founded in 2016 by international coach Rosanna Trigg, The Yard teaches children from 18 months to 16 years every week across four Singapore centres — from first classes to competitive squads.

Curriculum that's already proven

Our recreational levels run on a structured Skills Matrix with assessments three times a year — a progression framework we can adapt to school settings rather than invent from scratch.

Safeguarding-led, school-ready

A published Child Safe Sport Commitment, nut-free centres and FIG-certified apparatus — including a 20,000 sq ft arena at Jurong that already hosts school-linked events like the UWC Dragons Invitational and FOBISIA.

How it works

Three steps,
no hard sell

We'd rather scope carefully than promise generically — that's how programs survive past their first term.

1 · Tell us about your school

Email enquiries@theyard.com.sg with your school, ages, rough numbers and what you have in mind — even if it's just an idea.

2 · Scope it together

A conversation (and a site visit where useful) to settle format, venue, group sizes, term dates and safety requirements — then a written proposal.

3 · Launch and iterate

Our coaches deliver; we review with you as the term runs and adjust. Programs are built to improve, not just to start.

A note on honesty: we don't publish a menu of school packages or name partner schools here, because every engagement so far has been shaped case-by-case. If you'd like references relevant to your context, ask us in your enquiry.

Good to know

Schools FAQs

Does The Yard run gymnastics programs for schools?

Yes — The Yard works with schools on gymnastics and movement programs, from curriculum PE support and CCAs to holiday programs and new program launches. There's no off-the-shelf package: every engagement is scoped with the school first. Email enquiries@theyard.com.sg to start the conversation.

Do programs run on campus or at your centres?

Either, depending on what the program needs. Some formats suit a school hall; apparatus-based sessions suit our centres, which include a 20,000 sq ft FIG-certified arena at Jurong. The right venue is part of the scoping conversation.

What does a school program cost?

There's no published rate card because no two school programs are alike — cost depends on format, group sizes, venue and duration. We prepare a proposal after an initial conversation.

Is The Yard safeguarding-led?

Yes. The Yard is a safeguarding-led club with a published Child Safe Sport Commitment, and every centre is nut-free.
