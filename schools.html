Why The Yard

A club your school
can build on.

You need a partner whose standards you can check before a single pupil steps onto a mat. Here is what a school programme with The Yard stands on.

A decade of coaching children

Founded in 2016 by former international gymnast Rosanna Trigg, The Yard coaches children from 18 months to 16 years every week across four Singapore centres, from a first forward roll to the competitive squads.

A curriculum that already works

Every recreational level runs on a 20-skill Skills Matrix, with Bronze, Silver and Gold medals awarded three times a year, so pupils see their own progress and teachers can report it. The same framework adapts to a school setting.

Safeguarding-led and school-ready

A published Child Safe Sport Commitment, screened and reference-checked coaches, nut-free centres and FIG-certified apparatus, including the 17,500 square foot arena at Jurong.

How it works

Three steps, and a programme
built to last.

Careful scoping is how a programme survives past its first term, so the schools team asks questions first and promises later.

Step one

Tell us about your school

Email enquiries@theyard.com.sg with your school, the ages involved, rough numbers and what you have in mind, even if it is only an idea.

Step two

Scope it together

A conversation, and a site visit where useful, settles the format, venue, group sizes, term dates and safety requirements. A written proposal follows.

Step three

Launch, review and improve

Our coaches deliver the programme and review it with you as the term runs. Small steps compound, so the second block is better than the first.

There is no menu of school packages on this page and no list of partner schools, because each engagement so far has been shaped case by case and a school is named only with its agreement. Ask for references relevant to your context in your enquiry.

Good to know

Schools questions

Does The Yard run gymnastics programmes for schools?

Yes. The Yard works with schools on gymnastics and movement programmes, from curriculum PE support and CCAs to holiday programmes and excursions. Every engagement is scoped with the school first. Email enquiries@theyard.com.sg to start the conversation.

Do programmes run on campus or at your centres?

Either, depending on what the programme needs. Some formats suit a school hall; apparatus-based sessions suit our centres, which include a 17,500 square foot FIG-certified arena at Jurong. The right venue is part of the scoping conversation.

What does a school programme cost?

There is no published rate card because cost depends on format, group sizes, venue and duration. The schools team prepares a written proposal after an initial conversation.

Is The Yard safeguarding-led?

Yes. The Yard follows the Safe Sport Singapore framework, publishes a Child Safe Sport Commitment, screens and reference-checks every coach, and keeps every centre nut-free.

Can a school bring a class for a one-off visit?

Yes. Excursions and one-off coached sessions at a centre are one of the formats we scope with schools. Tell us the year group, the numbers and the date you have in mind.

Bring The Yard to school

Bring The Yard to school.

One email starts it. Tell us your school, the ages involved and what you are imagining, whether that is curriculum support, a CCA, a holiday programme or an excursion, and the schools team will come back with an honest read on what would work for your pupils.
