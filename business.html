Why The Yard

Every partnership stands
on the same club our
members train with.

The coaches, the apparatus and the four centres are the ones our members use every week, and the way we coach a six year old is the way we run a session for your team: foundations first, small steps that compound, and energy that everyone in the building is responsible for.

A decade of operating

Founded in 2016 by former international gymnast Rosanna Trigg after thirty years as a competitive gymnast and coach in the UK, Mexico and Singapore, and grown to four centres across the island.

Competition-grade facilities

FIG-certified apparatus from Gymnova and Gao Fei at every centre, and a 17,500 square foot arena at Jurong that hosts sanctioned meets.

Our own coaches run it

The coaches who teach our members all week run every corporate session, school visit and performance block, so the day is structured, safe and fun at any fitness level.

High-performance depth

A competitive WAG and MAG pathway on SGLP and USAG standards, training at the Jurong arena and competing at sanctioned meets.

How it starts

One email, one conversation,
then a written scope.

Corporate events are quoted on enquiry. Schools, consultancy and performance engagements take the same three steps and end with a proposal written for your situation.

Step one

Tell us who you are

Email enquiries@theyard.com.sg with your organisation, the people involved and what you have in mind, even if it is only an idea.

Step two

A conversation with the right person

Your enquiry goes to whoever leads that line of work. Where it helps, they will suggest a visit to a centre so you can see the standard we work to.

Step three

A proposal you can act on

Format, venue, dates and fees set out in writing, then delivered by our own coaches and reviewed with you as it runs.

Good to know

Partnership questions

Does The Yard work with organisations as well as families?

Yes. Corporate team building, brand activations and venue hire run at all four Singapore centres, and the team takes enquiries from schools, gym founders and sports clubs. Email enquiries@theyard.com.sg and it reaches the right person.

Which companies have held events at The Yard?

Past corporate clients include LinkedIn, TP ICAP and Chanel. Corporate events are coach-led and quoted on enquiry. See how corporate events work.

Is there a price list for schools, consultancy or performance work?

No. Every school programme, consultancy engagement and performance block is scoped from a conversation, because each one depends on the ages, numbers, venue and calendar involved. A written proposal follows that conversation.

Where do partnership sessions take place?

At any of the four centres (Jurong, Bukit Timah, Dempsey and Dover), including the 17,500 square foot arena at Jurong, or on your own site where the format suits it. The venue is settled during scoping. Compare the centres.
