The case for it

The mother
of all sports

Gymnastics has been called the mother of all sports for a reason: it builds the physical literacy every other sport draws on. Read the full evidence page →

Cross-sport transfer

Sports bodies including the FIG point to gymnastics' transfer into diving, martial arts, swimming, football, rugby, dance, climbing and athletics — through strength, mobility, body control and spatial awareness.

Long-term athlete development

Research on youth athletic development (including Lloyd et al. in the British Journal of Sports Medicine and American Academy of Pediatrics guidance) supports broad movement foundations for young athletes — the ground gymnastics training covers.

Falling, landing, flying

Body awareness in the air, controlled landings and confident falling are gymnastics bread-and-butter — and exactly the skills that protect and sharpen athletes in contact and aerial sports.

Conditioning in our DNA

Strength and conditioning blocks are already part of how we train our own squad athletes — this offering extends that craft to your club.

What a program can cover

Built with your coaches, not sold off a shelf

We don't publish a standard package, because a rugby academy, a dive squad and a dance company don't need the same program. Typical building blocks we'd scope with you:

  • Strength & conditioning blocks — short-cycle intensives or season-long support
  • Mobility, flexibility and body-control work on gymnastics apparatus
  • Landing mechanics, falling skills and aerial awareness (foam pits and trampolines earn their keep here)
  • Team sessions at our centres, formatted around your training calendar

Tell us your sport, squad size and season — we'll propose a format that fits.

How it works

Scoped like a
training plan

Good performance work starts from your sport's demands, not ours — so we start by listening.

1 · Tell us about the squad

Email enquiries@theyard.com.sg — your sport, athlete ages, squad size and what you want the block to achieve.

2 · Plan it with your coaches

A conversation between your coaching staff and ours to shape sessions around your season, training load and goals — ideally with a look around the arena.

3 · Run the block, review it

Sessions delivered by our coaches at our centres, reviewed with you as the block runs — kept, adjusted or extended on results.

A note on honesty: we don't publish club case studies or name teams here. If you'd like to talk to us about what we've run and for whom, ask in your enquiry — we'd rather tell you directly than dress it up on a webpage.

Good to know

Performance FAQs

Do you train athletes from other sports?

Yes — we offer gymnastics-based athletic performance training for competitive sports clubs and teams. There's no off-the-shelf program: each engagement is designed with the club's coaches and scoped from a conversation. Email enquiries@theyard.com.sg.

Which sports benefit from gymnastics-based training?

Gymnastics is widely regarded as a foundation sport: research and sports bodies including the FIG point to transfer into diving, martial arts, swimming, football, rugby, dance, climbing and athletics — through strength, mobility, body control and spatial awareness. See the evidence →

Where would sessions run?

Typically at our centres. The 20,000 sq ft FIG-certified arena at Jurong — home of our High Performance hub — suits team sessions, with sprung floors, foam pits and trampolines.

Do you take individual athletes rather than clubs?

This offering is built for clubs, squads and teams. If you're an individual athlete or parent, email enquiries@theyard.com.sg and the team will point you to the right option — often an existing Yard program.
