Move · Learning through motion, building your strength

Gymnastics is the mother
of all sports.

The name has stuck for a reason: gymnastics builds the physical literacy every other sport draws on. Read the full evidence page.

Cross-sport transfer

Sports bodies including the FIG point to gymnastics' transfer into diving, martial arts, swimming, football, rugby, dance, climbing and athletics, through strength, mobility, body control and spatial awareness.

Long-term athlete development

Research on youth athletic development, including Lloyd and colleagues in the British Journal of Sports Medicine and American Academy of Pediatrics guidance, supports broad movement foundations for young athletes, which is exactly the ground gymnastics training covers.

Falling, landing, flying

Body awareness in the air, controlled landings and confident falling are the bread and butter of gymnastics, and the same skills protect and sharpen athletes in contact and aerial sports.

Arena-grade facilities

A 17,500 square foot arena at Jurong with FIG-certified, competition-standard apparatus, sprung floors, foam pits and trampolines.

Coaches who compete

The same coaching team that prepares our gymnasts for sanctioned meets such as the Lion City Classic and the Diamond Classic, and for international tours.

Conditioning, every session

Strength and conditioning get real time in every Yard class, from FUNdamentals to squad, and the short-cycle blocks our squad athletes already run are the model for yours.

What a block can cover

Built with your coaches around your season

There is no standard package, because a rugby academy, a dive squad and a dance company need different things. These are the building blocks the coaches scope with you.

  • Strength and conditioning blocks, as short-cycle intensives or season-long support
  • Mobility, flexibility and body-control work on gymnastics apparatus
  • Landing mechanics, falling skills and aerial awareness, where foam pits and trampolines earn their keep
  • Team sessions at our centres, formatted around your training calendar

Tell the team your sport, squad size and season, and a format comes back that fits.

How it works

Scoped like a
training plan.

Good performance work starts from the demands of your sport, so the first conversation is with your coaches, and the listening comes before the plan.

Step one

Tell us about the squad

Email enquiries@theyard.com.sg with your sport, athlete ages, squad size and what you want the block to achieve.

Step two

Plan it with your coaches

A conversation between your coaching staff and ours shapes sessions around your season, training load and goals, ideally after a look around the arena.

Step three

Run the block, review it

Yard coaches deliver the sessions at our centres and review them with you as the block runs. Small steps compound, so the block is kept, adjusted or extended on what the results say.

No club case studies or team names appear on this page. Ask in your enquiry what The Yard has run and for whom, and the answer comes back directly.

Good to know

Performance questions

Do you train athletes from other sports?

Yes. The Yard offers gymnastics-based athletic performance training for competitive sports clubs and teams, and each engagement is designed with the club's coaches and scoped from a conversation. Email enquiries@theyard.com.sg.

Which sports benefit from gymnastics-based training?

Gymnastics is widely regarded as a foundation sport. Research and sports bodies including the FIG point to transfer into diving, martial arts, swimming, football, rugby, dance, climbing and athletics, through strength, mobility, body control and spatial awareness. See the evidence.

Where would sessions run?

Usually at our centres. The 17,500 square foot FIG-certified arena at Jurong, where our competitive squads train, suits team sessions, with sprung floors, foam pits and trampolines.

Do you take individual athletes as well as clubs?

This offering is built for clubs, squads and teams. If you are an individual athlete or a parent, email enquiries@theyard.com.sg and the team will point you to the right option, often an existing Yard programme.
