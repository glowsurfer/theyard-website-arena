The track record

We advise on it because
we have done it.

This page makes one claim: The Yard has built and runs real gyms. Every question you bring has been solved on one of these four floors first, usually the hard way.

Two clubs founded

Founder Rosanna Trigg, a former international gymnast, started Gym With Me in 2010 and The Yard in 2016 after thirty years as a competitive gymnast and coach in the UK, Mexico and Singapore.

Four centres opened

Jurong, Bukit Timah, Dempsey and Dover: different sites and different footprints, from a mall basement to a heritage building to a purpose-built arena.

A 17,500 square foot arena

The newest build, at Jurong, is fitted with FIG-certified, international-competition-standard apparatus and hosts sanctioned meets.

Programmes that fill them

Toddler classes through to invitation-only competitive squads, plus camps, parties and events, all run on one Skills Matrix and one booking system: the engine that keeps four centres full of members.

01 · Building a gym

From empty unit to opening day

The decisions that make or break a build, made four times over at four very different sites, with a mall basement, a heritage building and a purpose-built arena among them.

  • Feasibility and site realities: footprints, ceilings, floors and what a gymnastics fit-out demands
  • Fit-out, apparatus and equipment choices, from recreational set-ups to FIG competition standard
  • Safety and safeguarding practice designed in from the first drawing

02 · Growing a gym

From open to full

Opening is the easy part. The decade after it is where The Yard has done its learning, and its GROW pillar (developing character, skills for life) applies to a gym as much as to a gymnast: honest feedback, clear standards and small steps that compound.

  • Curriculum and the Skills Matrix: levels, assessments and progression families can see
  • Coach recruitment, training and retention for a children-first environment
  • Operations: class scheduling, booking systems, and camps, parties and events as revenue lines

How it starts

A conversation first,
then a scope.

Advisory work only helps when it is specific, so the front door is simple and the first call is honest.

Step one

Email us your project

Write to enquiries@theyard.com.sg with the idea, the site if you have one, the stage you are at and where you are stuck.

Step two

An honest first call

You talk the project through with the people who have done it, and you hear plainly where The Yard can add value. A walk around one of the centres is usually the fastest way to see the standard it works to.

Step three

A written scope

If it is a fit, you receive a scope and a fee written for your project, with no commitment beyond the work agreed.

Good to know

Consultancy questions

Does The Yard offer gym consultancy?

Yes. The Yard advises founders and operators who are building or growing a gymnastics facility, and each engagement is scoped from a conversation about the project. Email enquiries@theyard.com.sg to start.

What experience do you bring to a gym build?

First-hand operating experience. Founder Rosanna Trigg, a former international gymnast, has spent thirty years as a competitive gymnast and coach in the UK, Mexico and Singapore, founded Gym With Me in 2010 and The Yard in 2016, and has grown the club to four Singapore centres, including a 17,500 square foot FIG-certified arena at Jurong.

What does an engagement cost?

There is no published rate card. Scope and fees are proposed after an initial conversation about the project: its stage, its size and the help it actually needs.

Can we visit a Yard centre before deciding?

Yes, and the team encourages it. Seeing the Jurong arena or any of the other three centres is the fastest way to judge the standard The Yard works to. See the centres.
