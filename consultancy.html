The track record

We advise on it because
we've done it

This page makes one claim only: The Yard has built and runs real gyms. Everything we'd advise you on, we've had to solve for ourselves first.

Two clubs founded

Founder Rosanna Trigg started Gym With Me in 2010, then The Yard in 2016 — after 30+ years coaching internationally across the UK, Mexico and Singapore.

Four centres opened

Jurong, Bukit Timah, Dempsey and Dover — different sites, different footprints, from a mall basement to a heritage building to a purpose-built arena.

A 20,000 sq ft arena

Our newest build: the Jurong arena, fitted with FIG-certified, international-competition-standard apparatus — it hosts sanctioned meets.

Programs that fill them

From toddler classes to invitation-only competitive squads, plus camps, parties and events — the operating engine that keeps four centres busy.

  • Apparatus and equipment choices, from recreational setups to FIG competition standard

02 · Growing a gym

From open to thriving

Opening is the easy part. We've spent a decade on what comes after.

  • Program and curriculum structure — levels, assessments and progression families can see
  • Coach recruitment, training and retention for a kids-first environment
  • The operating layer: class scheduling, booking systems, camps, parties and events as revenue lines

How it starts

A conversation,
then a scope

Advisory only works when it's specific. We keep the front door simple.

1 · Email us your project

Write to enquiries@theyard.com.sg — the idea, the site (if you have one), the stage you're at and where you're stuck.

2 · An honest first call

We talk through the project and tell you plainly where we can add value and where we can't. Visiting our centres is usually the fastest way to see the standard we work to.

3 · A written scope

If it's a fit, we propose a scope and fee for your project — no standard package, no lock-in beyond the work agreed.

Good to know

Consultancy FAQs

Does The Yard offer gym consultancy?

Yes — we advise founders and operators who are building or growing a gymnastics facility. There's no fixed package: each engagement is scoped from a conversation about the project. Email enquiries@theyard.com.sg to start.

What experience do you bring to a gym build?

First-hand operating experience: founder Rosanna Trigg has coached internationally for over 30 years across the UK, Mexico and Singapore, founded Gym With Me in 2010 and The Yard in 2016, and has grown the club to four Singapore centres — including a 20,000 sq ft FIG-certified arena at Jurong.

What does an engagement cost?

There's no published rate card. Scope and fees are proposed after an initial conversation about the project — its stage, size and what help is actually needed.
