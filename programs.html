Every way to move at The Yard.

The Yard runs six programs across four Singapore centres: recreational gymnastics at every centre and a selection-based competitive WAG/MAG pathway at the Jurong High Performance hub, plus Ninja Zone (Bukit Timah & Dempsey), Tumbling & Trampoline (Jurong), Freestyle (Dover) and adult sessions (Jurong & Bukit Timah).

The athletic foundation for every sport your child will ever do — Gym Tots, KinderTots and FUNdamentals at every centre, progressing to Warriors (Bukit Timah & Dover), Senior Warriors (Dover) and boys' classes (Dempsey & Dover), with skills medalled Bronze, Silver and Gold three times a year.

Competitive WAG / MAG

The deep track: a selection-based artistic gymnastics pathway on SGLP and USAG standards. Squads train 6–24 hours a week over an August–July season and compete at the Lion City Classic, Diamond Classic and national meets. Entry starts with a free assessment — most athletes are first spotted in recreational classes.

Good to know

Choosing a program

Which program should my child start with?

Most children start in recreational gymnastics: Gym Tots (18 months–3, parent-and-child), KinderTots (3–4) or FUNdamentals (5–10) — all run at every Yard centre. High-energy 3–10 year olds at Bukit Timah or Dempsey can also try Ninja Zone, and 10–16 year olds at Jurong can start with Tumbling & Trampoline. No experience is needed and children can join at any time of year.

Which programs run at which centre?

Recreational gymnastics (Gym Tots, KinderTots, FUNdamentals) runs at all four centres. Warriors runs at Bukit Timah and Dover; Senior Warriors at Dover; boys' classes at Dempsey and Dover. Ninja Zone runs at Bukit Timah and Dempsey only. Tumbling & Trampoline runs at Jurong only. Freestyle is based at Dover. Adult sessions run at Jurong (Open Session) and Bukit Timah (Adult Foundation Gymnastics). The competitive WAG/MAG pathway is based at the Jurong High Performance hub — see the full table.

Can a child do more than one program?

Yes — children can combine disciplines, for example gymnastics plus ninja or tumbling, starting at the foundational level in each. The centre team can help build a weekly combination that fits.

How do I join the competitive squads?

Competitive WAG/MAG entry is by assessment and invitation, not open enrolment. The assessment is free and arranged with the centre team; most squad athletes are first noticed by their coach in recreational classes. How the pathway works →
