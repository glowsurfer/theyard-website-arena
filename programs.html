Programmes · The full line-up

Every way to move at The Yard

The Yard runs six programmes across four Singapore centres, and all ages and abilities are welcome: recreational gymnastics at every centre, a selection-based competitive WAG and MAG pathway at the Jurong High-Performance Arena, Ninja Zone at Bukit Timah and Dempsey, Tumbling and Trampoline at Jurong, Freestyle at Dover and adult sessions at Jurong and Bukit Timah.

The two programmes
everything else hangs off.

Recreational gymnastics is the backbone of the club, and it runs at all four centres from 18 months. The competitive pathway is where it can lead, by free assessment and invitation, and most squad athletes were first spotted by their coach in a recreational class.

All four centres18 months to 16 years

Recreational gymnastics

The athletic foundation for every sport your child will ever play, coached one small step at a time. Gym Tots, KinderTots and FUNdamentals run at every centre, progressing to Warriors (Bukit Timah and Dover), Senior Warriors (Dover) and the boys' classes (Dempsey and Dover), with skills medalled Bronze, Silver and Gold three times a year and every level move called by the coach.

Competitive WAG & MAG

A selection-based artistic gymnastics pathway on SGLP and USAG standards, for the children who want to take the sport seriously. Squads train 6 to 24 hours a week over an August to July season and compete at the Lion City Classic, the Diamond Classic and national meets. Entry starts with a free assessment, and placement is earned every week after it.

Ninja Zone

Gymnastics, parkour and obstacle-course training that turns boundless energy into strength and focus, on the custom-built rig at Bukit Timah, our Ninja Hub, and at Dempsey.

Tumbling and Trampoline

Round-offs to aerials on floor, tumble track and trampoline, a confidence sport for tweens and teens at the Jurong High-Performance Arena.

Freestyle

Gymnastics meets acrobatic self-expression at Dover, the All-Rounder, for children who want to move their own way. Camps are confirmed and weekly classes are on enquiry.

Adult gymnastics

An Open Session at Jurong with a coach always on the floor, and a coached Adult Foundation class at Bukit Timah. Beginners are welcome at both.

Good to know

Choosing a programme

Which programme should my child start with?

Most children start in recreational gymnastics: Gym Tots (18 months to 3, parent and child), KinderTots (3 to 4) or FUNdamentals (5 to 10), and all three run at every Yard centre. High-energy 3 to 10 year olds at Bukit Timah or Dempsey can also try Ninja Zone, and 10 to 16 year olds at Jurong can start with Tumbling and Trampoline. No experience is needed, all ages and abilities are welcome, and children can join at any time of year.

Which programmes run at which centre?

Recreational gymnastics (Gym Tots, KinderTots, FUNdamentals) runs at all four centres. Warriors runs at Bukit Timah and Dover; Senior Warriors at Dover; boys' classes at Dempsey and Dover. Ninja Zone runs at Bukit Timah and Dempsey. Tumbling and Trampoline runs at Jurong. Freestyle is based at Dover. Adult sessions run at Jurong (Open Session) and Bukit Timah (Adult Foundation Gymnastics). The competitive WAG and MAG pathway trains at The Yard Jurong. See the full table.

Can a child do more than one programme?

Yes. Children can combine disciplines, for example gymnastics plus ninja or tumbling, starting at the foundational level in each. The centre team can help build a weekly combination that fits.

How do I join the competitive squads?

Competitive WAG and MAG entry is by assessment and invitation. The assessment is free and arranged with the centre team, and most squad athletes are first noticed by their coach in recreational classes. How the pathway works.
