The Yard Bukit Timah

The Yard Bukit Timah is the Ninja Hub of the club, at KAP Mall, 9 King Albert Park, opposite King Albert Park MRT. A custom-built ninja rig stands beside a full gymnastics floor, so a child can start in Gym Tots at 18 months and move through FUNdamentals into Warriors, or take the Ninja Zone pathway from Ninja Tots to Ninja White and beyond. Adults have their own Foundation Gymnastics class here, and the club's dedicated party room is down the corridor.

Bukit Timah is where the club throws most of its parties, with a decorated room set aside for the cake and a choice of gymnastics or ninja for the coached hour and a half. In the holidays the rig works all week.

Ninja
The facility

Gymnastics and ninja
under one mall roof.

KAP Mall puts a full gymnastics floor, a purpose-built ninja rig and a party room a short lift ride from the MRT, which is why so many Bukit Timah families walk in. What they find inside is the same club as the other three centres: the same pathway, the same medals, and coaches who learn every child's name in the first week.

Custom-built ninja rig

A purpose-built obstacle rig for Ninja Zone classes, camps and parties, with bungee cords, a cargo net and ninja rings, and a 21-skill Ninja White matrix to work through on it.

Dedicated party room

The Yard centre with a room set aside for birthdays, decorated in your two chosen colours, with a fridge for the cake.

FIG-certified apparatus

The same coaching standards and competition-grade Gymnova and Gao Fei equipment as every Yard centre, so a level earned here is a level everywhere in the club.

Viewing area

Watch every class from the parent viewing area. The centre is nut-free throughout, and the coach is on the floor before the class starts.

Good to know

Bukit Timah questions

Which classes run at Bukit Timah?

Bukit Timah runs recreational gymnastics (Gym Tots for 18 months to 3, KinderTots for 3 to 4, FUNdamentals for 5 to 10 and Warriors for 5 to 10), Ninja Zone (Ninja Tots for 3 to 4 and Ninja White for 5 to 10) on a custom-built ninja rig, and Adult Foundation Gymnastics, a coached adult class for ages 16 and over.

Does Bukit Timah run Ninja Zone?

Yes. Bukit Timah is the club's Ninja Hub and one of the two Yard centres with Ninja Zone (the other is Dempsey). The custom-built rig is here, Ninja Tots (3 to 4) and Ninja White (5 to 10) are open for booking, and the Yellow and Green levels come by the coaches' invitation once a child has earned them. See Ninja Zone.

How do I get to Bukit Timah and where do I park?

The Yard Bukit Timah is at 9 King Albert Park, #B1-01, KAP Mall, Singapore 598332, opposite King Albert Park MRT. KAP Mall parking is paid at $1.60 an hour with a 10-minute grace period.

Can I hold a birthday party at Bukit Timah?

Yes. Bukit Timah has a dedicated birthday party room. Parties run for about two hours: a 90-minute coach-led gymnastics or ninja session followed by 30 minutes of cake-cutting in the decorated party room. Standard slots are Saturday and Sunday, 3:30 to 5:30pm, and Bukit Timah parties book online. See party packages.

Ready when you are

Book a trial class.

Book a trial at Bukit Timah for any recreational class, Warriors, Ninja Zone or Adult Foundation Gymnastics, and the fee is shown at booking. If you would rather talk it through first, the Bukit Timah team is a WhatsApp away and will recommend one class, the one that fits.
