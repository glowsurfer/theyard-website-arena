Bukit Timah FAQs

Which classes run at Bukit Timah?

Bukit Timah runs recreational gymnastics — Gym Tots (18 months–3), KinderTots (3–4), FUNdamentals (5–10) and Warriors (5–10) — plus Ninja Zone (Ninja Tots 3–4 and Ninja White 5–10) on a dedicated ninja rig, and Adult Foundation Gymnastics, a coached adult class (16+).

Does Bukit Timah run Ninja Zone?

Yes. Bukit Timah is one of only two Yard centres with Ninja Zone (the other is Dempsey), and it has a dedicated ninja rig. Ninja Tots (3–4) and Ninja White (5–10) are open for booking; Yellow and Green levels are by invitation. See Ninja Zone →

Where is The Yard Bukit Timah and how do I contact it?

9 King Albert Park, #B1-01, KAP Mall, Singapore 598332. Phone +65 6914 9669, WhatsApp +65 8952 8017, email enquiries.bukittimah@theyard.com.sg.

Can I hold a birthday party at Bukit Timah?

Yes — Bukit Timah has a dedicated birthday party room. Parties are about two hours: a 90-minute coach-led gymnastics or ninja session followed by 30 minutes of cake-cutting in the decorated party room. Standard slots are Saturday and Sunday 3:30–5:30pm. See party packages →
