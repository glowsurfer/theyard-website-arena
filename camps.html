Every school holiday · March, June, September and December

School holidays, well spent.

The Yard runs five-day holiday camps, Monday to Friday, three hours a day, in every Singapore school-holiday window. Gymnastics camps run at all four centres, Ninja Zone camps at Bukit Timah and Dempsey, High Performance competitive camps at the Jurong arena and a Freestyle camp at Dover. For many families a camp week is their first taste of the club.

Play · Fueled with fun, designed for progress

A full week of
real coaching.

The same coaches, the same safety standards and the same culture as our weekly classes, in structured sessions on FIG-certified apparatus. Fun is designed into every hour, and over five consecutive days children settle in, make friends and visibly build.

The perfect first taste

For many families a camp is their first contact with The Yard: a fun, low-commitment way to try the club for a week before joining a weekly class and becoming a member.

Mixed ability, by design

Camp groups deliberately mix abilities, with skill development alongside games and broader physical play, so beginners and regulars both get a great week.

Top-up or way in

Members use camps to keep moving through the holidays and new families use them as a way in. Camps complement weekly classes, which is where the Skills Matrix, the medals and the coach-called level moves happen.

The format

Five mornings make one big week

Each camp day is three hours of coached activity: warm-up, apparatus rotations, skills and games, then a cool-down. By Wednesday the shy ones are leading the warm-up, and by Friday the whole group has something new to show you, because small steps compound quickly when they come five days in a row.

  • Camps are booked as full five-day weeks. Partial weeks open only on the Friday before camp week starts, subject to space.
  • Fees are shown at booking, per centre and per camp week, and camp bookings are non-refundable.
  • Bring a water bottle, nut-free snacks, close-fitting sportswear without zips and a change of clothes.
  • Children train barefoot, so no socks during gymnastics.

Good to know

Camp questions

When do The Yard's holiday camps run?

Camps run in every Singapore school-holiday window, in March, June, September and December, as five-day weeks from Monday to Friday with three hours of coached activity a day. Weekly classes keep running through the holidays too, so camps are a top-up alongside them.

Does my child need gymnastics experience to join a camp?

No. Gymnastics, Ninja and Freestyle camps are deliberately mixed ability, every child within the age band is welcome, and many families use a camp as their first taste of The Yard before joining weekly classes. High Performance competitive camps are the exception, as they serve squad-level athletes at Jurong.

Which camps run at which centre?

Gymnastics camps for the KinderTots and FUNdamentals age bands run at all four centres: Jurong, Bukit Timah, Dempsey and Dover. Ninja Zone camps run at Bukit Timah and Dempsey. High Performance competitive camps run at Jurong. The Freestyle camp runs at Dover.

How do I book a camp?

Booking is self-serve on each centre's online booking page, and camp fees are shown at booking. If you are unsure which camp fits your child, WhatsApp the centre team and they will help you choose.

Can I book single days instead of the full week?

Camps are booked as full five-day weeks. Partial-week bookings open only on the Friday before the camp week starts, subject to space. Camp bookings are non-refundable, so pick your week carefully.

What should my child bring to camp?

A water bottle, snacks (every Yard centre is nut-free), close-fitting sportswear with no zips, and a change of clothes. Children train barefoot, so no socks during gymnastics.
