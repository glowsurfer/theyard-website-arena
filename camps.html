Why a Yard camp

A full week of
real coaching

Same coaches, same safety standards and same culture as our term classes — structured sessions on FIG-certified apparatus, not a free-for-all.

The perfect first taste

For many families a camp is their first contact with The Yard — a fun, low-commitment way to try the club for a week before joining term classes.

Mixed ability, by design

Camp groups deliberately mix abilities — skill development plus games and broader physical play, so beginners and regulars both get a great week.

Top-up or entry point

Members use camps to keep moving through the holidays; new families use them as a way in. Either way, camps complement term classes — curriculum progression happens in weekly classes.

The format

Five mornings,
one big week

Each camp day is 3 hours of coached activity: warm-up, apparatus rotations, skills and games, cool-down. Over five consecutive days children settle in, make friends and visibly build — the week itself is the progression.

  • Book the week — camps are 5-day bookings; partial weeks only open on the Friday before camp week starts, subject to space
  • Fees shown at booking — per centre, per camp week; camp bookings are non-refundable
  • Bring: water bottle · nut-free snacks · close-fitting sportswear (no zips) · a change of clothes
  • No socks during gymnastics — children train barefoot

Good to know

Camp FAQs

When do The Yard's holiday camps run?

Camps run in every Singapore school-holiday window — March, June, September and December — as 5-day weeks, Monday to Friday, 3 hours per day. Regular term classes keep running through the holidays too, so camps are a top-up, not a replacement.

Does my child need gymnastics experience to join a camp?

No. Gymnastics, Ninja and Freestyle camps are deliberately mixed-ability — many families use a camp as their first taste of The Yard before joining term classes. High Performance competitive camps are the exception: they serve squad-level athletes at Jurong.

Which camps run at which centre?

Gymnastics camps (KinderTots and FUNdamentals age bands) run at all four centres — Jurong, Bukit Timah, Dempsey and Dover. Ninja Zone camps run at Bukit Timah and Dempsey. High Performance competitive camps run at Jurong. The Freestyle camp runs at Dover.

How do I book a camp?

Booking is self-serve per centre: Jurong and Bukit Timah camps are booked on ClassCard; Dover and Dempsey camps are currently booked through the iClassPro parent portal (moving to ClassCard). Camp fees are shown at booking. If you're not sure which camp fits your child, WhatsApp the centre team.

Can I book single days instead of the full week?

Camps are booked as full 5-day weeks. Partial-week bookings only open on the Friday before the camp week starts, subject to space. Camp bookings are non-refundable, so pick your week carefully.

What should my child bring to camp?

A water bottle, snacks (every Yard centre is strictly nut-free), close-fitting sportswear with no zips, and a change of clothes. Children train barefoot — no socks during gymnastics.
