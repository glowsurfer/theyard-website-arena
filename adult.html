Adult gymnastics

It is never too late to get on the gym floor. The Yard runs two adult gymnastics programmes for ages 16 and over: a 90-minute Open Session at Jurong with a coach always on the floor, and Adult Foundation Gymnastics, a coached class at Bukit Timah. No experience is needed at either, and our adult members range from returning gymnasts to complete beginners.

Move · Learning through motion, building your strength

Movement on
your own terms.

Adults leave The Yard physically stronger, mentally lighter and reconnected with the kind of play a desk and a treadmill never give you. Some of our adult members did gymnastics as children, most are trying it for the first time, and the floor is welcoming either way.

Returning gymnasts

Did gymnastics as a child? The full FIG-standard apparatus is here: vault, bars, beam, floor and trampoline, so you can pick your old skills back up safely and at your own pace.

Curious beginners

Parkour-curious, calisthenics-strong, or simply done with the same three machines. Learn handstands, rolls and swings from scratch with a coach on hand, one small step at a time.

An unusual way to stay strong

Gymnastics builds whole-body strength, mobility and coordination, and because it feels like play you will never have to talk yourself into turning up.

Open Session · Jurong Adult Foundation · Bukit Timah

Open Session: adult open gym

Ages 16 and over1.5 hoursSelf-directed, coach always on the floor

A 90-minute self-directed session across the full apparatus at the Jurong arena. You set the plan and the pace, and a qualified coach is always on the floor to supervise, give safety advice and teach skills whenever you ask.

What a session looks like

Warm up your own way, then use vault, bars, beam, floor and trampoline as you like. Drill a handstand, chase a kip, bounce out the working week. The coach on the floor will spot you, set up progressions and answer questions on request.

Who it suits

Returning gymnasts, parkour-curious beginners and anyone who wants an unusual way to stay strong. Complete beginners are welcome, and the coach will get you started safely with the foundations first.

Adult Foundation Gymnastics

Ages 16 and overCoached classStructured, foundations first

A structured, coach-led adult class at Bukit Timah for anyone who prefers a plan to a blank floor. Sessions work through gymnastics foundations as a group: strength and flexibility work first, then skills across the apparatus, built up in coached progressions, because foundations come before sophistication at every age.

Who it suits

Adults who want the guidance of a proper class, with a coach setting the session, correcting technique and moving everyone through progressions week to week. It suits you if you are starting from zero or want structure to rebuild old skills properly.

Class times, duration and fees are confirmed by the Bukit Timah team when you enquire.

Good to know

Adult programme questions

Do I need gymnastics experience to join an adult session?

No. All abilities are welcome at both adult programmes. At the Jurong Open Session a coach is always on the floor to keep things safe, offer advice and teach skills on request, and Adult Foundation Gymnastics at Bukit Timah is a coached class built around the foundations.

What is the difference between the Open Session and Adult Foundation?

The Open Session at Jurong is a 90-minute self-directed open gym with no group warm-up or class plan. You work on the skills you want at your own pace with a coach always on the floor. Adult Foundation Gymnastics at Bukit Timah is a structured, coach-led class that works through gymnastics foundations as a group.

Which Yard centres run adult programmes?

Adult programmes currently run at two centres: Jurong for the Open Session and Bukit Timah for Adult Foundation Gymnastics. Dempsey and Dover have no regular adult classes at present.

How old do I have to be?

Adult programmes are for ages 16 and over. Teens aged 10 to 16 who want structured training can look at Tumbling and Trampoline at Jurong or Senior Warriors at Dover.

How do I book an adult session?

Message the centre on WhatsApp: Jurong for the Open Session and Bukit Timah for Adult Foundation Gymnastics. The team confirms current session times, availability and the per-session fee, and books you in.

What should I wear and bring?

Fitted sportswear you can move freely in, with hair tied back and no jewellery. Gymnastics is done in bare feet or gymnastics shoes. Bring a water bottle, and remember that every Yard centre is nut-free.
