Adult Gymnastics

The Yard runs two adult gymnastics programs for ages 16+: a 90-minute Open Session (open gym) at Jurong with a qualified coach always on the floor, and Adult Foundation Gymnastics, a coached class at Bukit Timah. No experience needed at either.

Why adults come

Movement on
your own terms

Adults leave The Yard physically stronger, mentally lighter, and reconnected with the kind of play they don't get at the desk — or the regular gym.

Returning gymnasts

Did gymnastics as a kid? Full FIG-standard apparatus — vault, bars, beam, floor, trampoline — to pick your old skills back up safely, at your own pace.

Curious beginners

Parkour-curious, calisthenics-strong or just done with treadmills — learn handstands, rolls and swings from scratch with a coach on hand.

An unusual way to stay strong

Gymnastics builds whole-body strength, mobility and coordination — and it's play, not a workout you have to talk yourself into.

Open Session · Jurong Adult Foundation · Bukit Timah

Open Session — adult open gym

Ages 16+1.5 hoursSelf-directed · coach always on the floor

A 90-minute self-directed session across the full apparatus at our Jurong arena — work on the skills you want, at the pace you want. There's no group warm-up and no class plan, but a qualified coach is always on the floor to supervise, give safety advice and teach skills whenever you ask.

What a session looks like

Warm up your own way, then use vault, bars, beam, floor and trampoline as you like. Drill a handstand, chase a kip, bounce out the work week — the coach on the floor will spot you, set up progressions and answer questions on request.

Who it suits

Returning gymnasts, parkour-curious beginners, and anyone who wants an unusual way to stay strong. Complete beginners are welcome — the coach will get you started safely.

Adult Foundation Gymnastics

Ages 16+Coached classStructured, foundations-first

A structured, coach-led adult class at Bukit Timah for anyone who prefers a plan to a blank floor. Sessions work through gymnastics foundations as a group — strength and flexibility work, then skills across apparatus, built up in coached progressions.

Who it suits

Adults who want the guidance of a proper class: a coach setting the session, correcting technique and moving you through progressions week to week. Ideal if you're starting from zero — or want structure to rebuild old skills properly.

Class times, duration and fees are confirmed by the Bukit Timah team when you enquire.

Adult program FAQs

Do I need gymnastics experience to join an adult session?

No. Beginners are welcome at both adult programs. At the Jurong Open Session a coach is always on the floor to keep things safe, offer advice and teach skills on request, and Adult Foundation Gymnastics at Bukit Timah is a coached class built around foundations — no prior experience needed.

What's the difference between Open Session and Adult Foundation?

The Open Session at Jurong is a 90-minute self-directed open gym: there's no group warm-up or class plan — you work on the skills you want, at your own pace, with a coach always on the floor. Adult Foundation Gymnastics at Bukit Timah is a structured, coach-led class that works through gymnastics foundations as a group.

Which Yard centres run adult programs?

Adult programs currently run at two centres: Jurong (Open Session, open gym) and Bukit Timah (Adult Foundation Gymnastics). There are no regular adult classes at Dempsey or Dover.

How old do I have to be?

Adult programs are for ages 16 and up. Teens aged 10–16 who want structured training can look at Tumbling & Trampoline at Jurong or Senior Warriors at Dover instead.

How do I book an adult session?

Message the centre on WhatsApp — Jurong for the Open Session and Bukit Timah for Adult Foundation Gymnastics. The team will confirm current session times, availability and the per-session fee, and get you booked in.

What should I wear and bring?

Comfortable sportswear you can move freely in; gymnastics is done barefoot or in grip socks. Bring a water bottle — and note all Yard centres are strictly nut-free.
